DAY 1 · TEACHER MODE

Kapitel 1: Und was machst du?

01 · WARM-UP

Let's understand the topic

In this chapter, you practise talking about what you studied, what work you do, and important experiences in your life.

Was machst du beruflich?
What do you do for a living?
Ich mache eine Ausbildung.
I am doing vocational training.
02 · GRAMMAR

Perfekt — talking about the past

Use the Perfekt to talk about completed past events in everyday spoken German.

Subject + haben/sein + … + Partizip II

The helping verb (haben/sein) is in position 2. The Partizip II usually goes at the end.

Ich habe Deutsch gelernt.
I learned German.
Ich bin nach Berlin gefahren.
I travelled to Berlin.

How to choose haben or sein?

  • haben: most verbs, especially with an object — Ich habe gearbeitet.
  • sein: commonly movement from one place to another or a change of state — Ich bin gefahren. / Ich bin aufgewacht.
03 · FORMATION

How Partizip II is formed

Regular: lernen → gelernt
-ieren verbs: renovieren → renoviert (no ge-)
Inseparable prefix: besuchen → besucht (no ge-)
Irregular: schreiben → geschrieben
04 · DRY RUN

Build the sentence step by step

1. SubjectIch
2. Helping verbhabe
3. Other informationgestern Deutsch
4. Partizip II at the endgelernt.

Ich habe gestern Deutsch gelernt.

05 · CHECK YOURSELF

06 · WRAP-UP

What you should remember

  • Perfekt is used frequently in spoken German for the past.
  • Use haben with most verbs; sein with many movement/change-of-state verbs.
  • In a main clause, Partizip II goes at the end.
